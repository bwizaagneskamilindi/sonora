
# SONORA
### Your music. Your library. Your control.

SONORA is a personal music library management platform designed to bring fragmented music collections together in one organized, accessible space.

By integrating with Spotify, SONORA aims to help users consolidate their playlists into a unified music catalogue, identify duplicate tracks, enrich music metadata, and discover legitimate sources for purchasing downloadable music.

Beyond streaming-based playlist management, SONORA focuses on personal music ownership, helping users manage acquired MP3 files and build organized offline music collections independent of any single streaming platform.

---

## Project Vision

Music collections are often scattered across multiple playlists, streaming services and platforms. SONORA aims to simplify this experience by providing a centralized space where users can manage, organize and preserve their music collections.

The project explores the intersection of music streaming APIs, data management, third-party integrations and local file management.

Its long-term vision is to give users greater control over how they organize, acquire and access their music.

## Core Features

The following features represent the project's development roadmap.

- **Spotify Integration:** Authenticate with Spotify and retrieve personal playlists through the Spotify Web API.
- **Playlist Consolidation:** Combine tracks from multiple playlists into one unified music catalogue.
- **Duplicate Detection:** Identify duplicate recordings and maintain a clean music library.
- **Music Metadata:** Retrieve and organize track, artist and album information.
- **Music Discovery:** Find legitimate purchasing sources for downloadable music.
- **MP3 Library Management:** Manage and organize legally acquired audio files.
- **Acquisition Tracking:** Track purchased, available and missing music.
- **Offline Collection:** Build a structured personal music collection accessible outside streaming platforms.

## Design Philosophy

SONORA uses a custom dark visual identity called **Cherry Noir**.

The design combines deep charcoal backgrounds, muted burgundy surfaces, rich cherry accents and soft rose highlights.

The interface is intended to feel:

- Elegant and expressive.
- Dark but visually engaging.
- Minimal without feeling empty.
- Functional without sacrificing personality.

## Development Approach

The application will be developed incrementally, following a structured engineering process:

1. Requirements analysis and system design.
2. Frontend architecture and interface development.
3. Backend implementation and API design.
4. Database modelling and integration.
5. Spotify authentication and playlist retrieval.
6. Music catalogue processing and consolidation.
7. Legitimate music acquisition integrations.
8. Local MP3 management.
9. Testing, security and deployment.

Each major feature will be designed, implemented, tested and documented before moving to the next stage.

## Copyright and Music Acquisition

SONORA does not extract, download or convert audio streams from Spotify.

Spotify serves as a source of playlist and track metadata. Downloadable music must be obtained through legitimate purchasing or distribution channels, subject to their licensing and usage conditions.

The application is intended to support lawful personal music collection management.
